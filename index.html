How It Works

From walk-in to your first PR.

Every plan starts with where your body actually is today
01

Movement Assessment

Before we load a bar, a coach checks your squat, hinge, and press patterns to flag anything that needs fixing first.

02

Baseline Numbers

We establish your current working weights across the main lifts, so progress is measured against you, not a stranger's program.

03

Written Program

A block built around your schedule and goal, powerlifting, general strength, or conditioning, updated every four weeks.

04

Weekly Check-In

Your coach reviews lift videos and adjusts loading before fatigue turns into a plateau or an injury.
